Important disclosures

• This is a business-to-business tool for legitimate commercial purposes only. It is not a consumer report under the Fair Credit Reporting Act (FCRA).

• Phone numbers may appear on the National Do Not Call Registry or state DNC lists. You are responsible for complying with the Telephone Consumer Protection Act (TCPA), FCC rules, and applicable state laws before calling or texting.

• We can provide known litigator scrubbing and DNC screening as part of the calling add-on. Screening reduces risk but does not eliminate it. You must still honor opt-outs and maintain your own suppression lists.

• Calling and texting features will use a third-party communications provider. Recording consent rules vary by state (one-party vs. two-party). Check your state laws.

• Always confirm you have a valid business purpose and any required consent before outreach.
